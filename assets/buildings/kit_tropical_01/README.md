# Tropical exterior kit 01 — draft

Nine independent 1024 × 1024 transparent PNGs. Illustrated cream plaster, teal wood, terracotta roofs. Art only: not registered with the editor or renderer yet. Existing assets and gameplay are unchanged.

Files in assets/buildings/kit_tropical_01/: bld_wall_plain_01.png, bld_wall_window_01.png, bld_wall_door_01.png, bld_roof_01.png, bld_window_01.png, bld_door_01.png, bld_restaurant_exterior_01.png, bld_storage_exterior_01.png.

Paving: assets/tiles/kit_tropical_01/tile_paving_sandstone_01.png. Diamond 1024 × 512 centered vertically; repeat-edge texture matching is NOT verified.

All objects are centered and bottom aligned at y=971, minimum 5% canvas margin. Canvas bounds are NOT a footprint definition. Wall endpoints, world size, pivot, supported orientations and connections still need calibration before modular placement. Wall ends currently have outlines and posts: do not claim seamless joins. Standalone window and door do not yet share a measured opening/socket size. Roof is one fixed illustrated roof, not an auto-expanding roof system. Generated from one atlas and extracted; increased image dimensions do not add native source detail.

Do not load this folder automatically into production. Keep as an art draft until map editor contract and in-game placement review are complete. Owner requested to arrange the entire map personally; gameplay ownership of NPC buildings remains a separate decision.
